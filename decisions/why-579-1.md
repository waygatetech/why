## Question

tix rejects unknown keys on plan decisions (no `ruling` field). What is the ruling for a plan entry?

## Ruling

the entry's `recommend`, as it stands when tix approve runs

## Why

No tix change, and the approve hook runs only after a human approval, so every entry is ruled by then. The workflow already says to write rulings back into the plan; this just names where. Body-prose parsing is fragile.
